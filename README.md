### About

I provide forensic consulting and technical assistance for criminal defense attorneys, law firms, and legal teams in cases where digital evidence is decisive.

My role as a **Technical Assistant in Expert Reports** (art. 159, §4º, Brazilian Code of Criminal Procedure) is to audit forensic reports, validate chain of custody, and deliver technical opinions that are defensible in court — ensuring that digital evidence meets methodological and legal standards before it reaches cross-examination.

I operate strictly within legal and ethical boundaries, following **ISO/IEC 27037:2012** and **NIST SP 800-86**, with SHA-256 hash verification on all evidence handled.

### Legal Framework

My work is grounded in Brazilian criminal procedural law and international forensic standards:

- **CPP arts. 158–182** (Expert evidence), **arts. 158-A–158-F** (Chain of custody, Lei 13.964/2019)
- **AgRg no HC 943.895/PR** (STJ) — burden of proving digital evidence integrity lies with the State
- **Rcl 92.830/MG** (STF) + **Súmula Vinculante 14** — defense right to full technical access to digital evidence
- **ISO/IEC 27037:2012**, **NIST SP 800-86**, **LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018)**
